EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

CivicConnect is a comprehensive, AI-powered civic complaint management platform
designed for the Mumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC). The system enables citizens
to report municipal issues across all 24 wards and allows administrators to
efficiently manage and track complaint resolution.

KEY ACHIEVEMENTS:
✅ 87 API endpoints fully implemented and tested
✅ 3-tier architecture (Frontend, Backend, AI Service)
✅ Real-time dashboard with live complaint statistics
✅ Role-based access control for 4 user types
✅ Complete coverage of 24 Mumbai wards
✅ Multi-language support (English & Marathi)
✅ AI-powered automatic complaint routing
